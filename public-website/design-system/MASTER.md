# Athenaeum public reading room

## Type and color

Set display text and book titles in Instrument Serif, regular and italic. Manrope variable handles continuous text, navigation and forms. Three local Latin WOFF2 files ship through Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License. Preload the upright serif to stabilize the first heading.

| Role | Size | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage title | 38–46px on phones, up to 88px on desktop | Close leading, upright opening and one rust italic phrase |
| Page title | 40–67px | Use the content width to shape the line |
| Section heading | 34–46px | Regular serif |
| Guide and service heading | 26–32px | Compact serif, aligned to its explanation |
| Book listing | 19px | The full title remains visible outside the cover |
| Reading text | 15–16px | 1.55–1.65 line height |
| Navigation and actions | 14px | Medium-weight sans serif |
| Captions and availability | 13px | Use words for status |

Warm paper (#faf8f1) and forest ink (#223a32) carry most of the page. Rust (#91452c) marks editorial italics and guide numbers. Sage (#e8ecdf) sets off the membership invitation. Thin neutral rules (#ccc9bd) separate subjects. The inked cover colors belong to the books.

## Composition

Keep the page within 1500px and use fluid outer margins of 20–72px. The hero uses a wide reading-table crop. A paper text panel overlaps its left edge on desktop and its bottom edge on phones. Keep the main action beside the introduction. The catalog shelf pairs each cover with its bibliographic information on desktop and stacks it below on phones. Use the title and author from the catalog when cover art is unavailable.

Use a left search column on Discover above 900px and a stacked form below. Align the guide and FAQ headings beside their entries where space allows. Reserve the shelf’s loading height so text beneath it stays steady. The footer holds secondary destinations.

## Interaction

Keep the cover hinge rotation within seven degrees and the link arrow movement within three pixels. A short downward entrance accompanies the mobile menu. Every action works immediately. Preserve keyboard focus rings, skip navigation, native form and disclosure behavior, and the Escape key. Disable motion transitions and cover rotation when reduced motion is requested.
